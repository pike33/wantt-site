First-time setup

Put Wantt in your
Share Sheet.

Wantt may not appear in the first row of apps straight away. Set it up once and it’ll be much easier to reach whenever you find something worth saving on Instagram.

01

Open a post or Reel, then tap Share

Start in Instagram. Find something you want to save, then tap the Share icon.

02

Choose Edit and find Wantt

At the bottom of the app list, tap Edit. Then look for Wantt.

03

Add Wantt to Favorites

Turn Wantt on. Move it closer to the front if you like, then tap Done.

You’re set

That’s it.

Next time you share something from Instagram, Wantt should be much easier to reach.
