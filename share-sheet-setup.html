    Save from Instagram.

    Watch the full example or follow these steps on your iPhone.

    1. Open a public Instagram post or Reel. Tap the share arrow, then Share to… if it appears.
    2. Swipe along the iPhone app row and choose Wantt.
    3. If it is missing, open More. Choose Wantt there, or use Edit to enable it if it is listed but off. Finish editing and choose Wantt. Favourites are optional.
    4. Wait for Saved to Wantt, then tap Done. You can keep browsing Instagram.
    5. Later, open Wantt. Resolved results appear in Places; check Recent saves while details are still processing or for links-only saves.
    6. Open a place for its available details and Things to try.

    Optional sharing shortcut.

    1. In the sharing app row, open More, then Edit.
    2. Tap the + beside Wantt to add it to Favourites when offered.
    3. If you want, hold its reorder handle and move Wantt nearer the top.
    4. Finish editing with the top-right checkmark or Done, then close the Apps list using the separate top-left checkmark or Done. Labels may vary.

    Watching does not configure your iPhone. Processing time and available details vary.

    Still can’t see Wantt?

    Make sure Wantt is installed. In More → Edit, enable Wantt if it is listed but off. Close and reopen the Share Sheet and try again. Wantt cannot add or enable itself automatically. Opening Wantt once after installation can help make it available to share to.
