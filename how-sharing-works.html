Come back to a Place

Wantt works quietly after you save. When it has enough evidence, it gives you a useful Place and keeps the original post as its source.

While we are in beta

A few useful things to know

  • Wantt currently works with public Instagram posts and Reels shared through iPhone’s Share Sheet.
  • Not every post contains enough reliable information to identify a Place. When it is unsure, Wantt may ask for help rather than guess.
  • Instagram content can change or disappear. Wantt is designed to preserve the useful result of your save, not to guarantee permanent access to the original post.
  • Your Saves are private to your Wantt account. Read more in Privacy.
