# Share Sheet setup

The guide has two separate lessons. The first-save lesson runs for 36.5 seconds
across six chapters. The optional Favourites lesson runs for 20 seconds across
three chapters. Both use reviewed picture sources, Rachel's cleaned narration,
the approved warm music and cues aligned with the demonstrated actions. The
swipe follows the recorded app row. The optional reorder illustrates holding
the actual three-line handle and moving the Wantt row between reviewed states.

Press Play once to watch a lesson through. The segmented progress bar follows
the playhead, and the left and right sides seek to the previous and next chapter
within the same video. Back, Next and arrow keys offer equivalent controls.
Holding a side pauses playback until release. The lesson ends on its final frame.
Choosing the optional lesson is a separate action.

Each full video contains its own narration, cues and continuous music bed.
Sound off mutes the whole mix. The two videos are identical to the native app's
bundled copies. Chapter starts and media hashes are in the asset manifest.

A local checkpoint resumes paused. Previous web chapter checkpoints migrate to
the equivalent position in the full video. Backgrounding pauses playback.
Captions, still viewing, Reduce Motion, written instructions and native video
controls when JavaScript is unavailable remain available.

On mobile the video fills the width between small side rails. The layout can
scroll to keep playback controls and instructions reachable on shorter screens.
